# Notices

## MaleCNS v1.0 connectome (data)

`data/mb_right.npz`, `data/mb_right_manifest.json` and `web/data/cells.json` are
derived from the MaleCNS v1.0 connectome of the adult male *Drosophila*
central nervous system (HHMI Janelia, Google Research, University of Cambridge,
MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology and collaborators; Berg et al., Cell 2026),
licensed under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

Changes: the right mushroom body (projection neurons, Kenyon cells, output
neurons) and its PAM/PPL1 dopaminergic inputs were selected and reduced to
synapse-count matrices and cell-body positions.

Source files:
https://storage.googleapis.com/flyem-male-cns/v1.0/connectome-data/flat-connectome/

## fly-blackjack (method)

The plasticity rule, the valence derivation from dominant dopaminergic input and
the T-maze style choice follow fly-blackjack by William Jones (MIT License):
https://github.com/WilliamJones/fly-blackjack
